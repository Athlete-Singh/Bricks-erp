Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TVAM KEY SOFTWARE PRIVATE LIMITED (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and safeguards information through the BRICS ERP platform and the BRICS mobile application (Package Name: com.bricks.user).

1. Information We Collect

BRICS is an offline-first enterprise application for brick kiln (bhatta) owners and field supervisors. We collect only data necessary to operate kiln business workflows:

  • Account Credentials: Mobile phone number, supervisor name, encrypted password, and assigned kiln role.
  • Field Operations: Daily brick production metrics (moulding, drying, chamber firing, nikasi sorting), labour attendance muster rolls, wage piece-rates, advances, goods receipt notes (GRN), and gate-out delivery challans.
  • Device & Connectivity: Device model, OS version, and network status to facilitate offline SQLite/Drift synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data is used solely for:

  • Maintaining accurate brick inventory and double-entry accounting ledgers.
  • Synchronizing on-site offline field entries with central servers.
  • Generating GST invoices, loading slips, and gate-out challans.
  • Calculating piece-rate wages and advance balances for labour groups.

We do NOT sell, rent, or monetize your data with third-party advertisers.

3. Device Permissions Used

  • INTERNET: Securely synchronizes field records with central servers over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects connectivity changes to trigger automatic background synchronization.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Delivers critical operational alerts and approvals.

4. Data Security & Multi-Tenancy

All operational and financial records are protected by strict company multi-tenant isolation, HTTPS encryption in transit, and role-based access control (RBAC).

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Organizations and users may request account and personal data deletion by visiting our Account Deletion Page or by emailing privacy@tvamkey.com. Certain financial audit records are retained under statutory GST regulations for up to 7 years.
