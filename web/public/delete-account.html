Google Play Account Deletion Policy

Request Account & Data Deletion

Follow the steps below to request deletion of your BRICS account (Package: com.bricks.user) and associated personal data.

How to Submit a Deletion Request

Send an email from your registered email address or phone number to:

privacy@tvamkey.com

Include the following details:

  • Registered Mobile Number or Email
  • Your Brick Kiln / Organization Name
  • Statement: "I request full deletion of my user account and personal data from BRICS."
Send Deletion Request Email

Data Retention & Deletion Details

Permanently Deleted: Profile credentials, mobile number, active session tokens, push tokens, and local offline cache.

Retained for Statutory Compliance: In accordance with Indian tax laws (GST and Companies Act), historical sales invoices, payment vouchers, and audit logs are retained by the kiln organization for statutory compliance up to 7 years.
