BRICS is a purpose-built, offline-first enterprise operations and field management app for brick kilns (bhatta) and chimney businesses.

Designed specifically for field conditions with intermittent or zero internet connectivity, BRICS enables supervisors and managers to capture real-time kiln operations on-site, syncing automatically with the central ERP as soon as connectivity is restored.

KEY FEATURES:

• PRODUCTION LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT:
Track every stage of brick production:
- Green brick moulding & pathai records
- Drying yard tracking
- Firing batch initialization, chamber temperature & fuel monitoring
- Unloading (nikasi) and sorting by brick quality grades (Class 1, Class 2, Class 3, Broken)

• LABOUR ATTENDANCE & ADVANCES:
- Real-time muster roll and daily attendance for kiln workers
- Sardar & labour group tracking
- Piece-rate accounting and advance management

• DISPATCH & VEHICLE GATE-PASS:
- Fast loading slip and delivery challan issuance directly at the loading ramp
- Vehicle gate-out tracking with truck number and driver details
- Zero unbilled truck leaks with instant stock deductions

• GOODS RECEIPT NOTE (GRN):
- Inward raw material recording for coal, firewood, soil, and fuel trucks
- Weighbridge integration and supplier challan verification

• OFFLINE-FIRST ARCHITECTURE:
- High-performance local database powered by Drift
- Full functionality without active internet
- Automatic background replay and conflict-free syncing with the central ERP backend

• ROLE-BASED ACCESS & AUDIT:
- Multi-kiln switching for business owners
- Secure JWT authentication with role-based permissions
- Tamper-proof activity logs for complete financial and stock integrity

Transform your kiln operations, eliminate leakage, and streamline production with BRICS.
