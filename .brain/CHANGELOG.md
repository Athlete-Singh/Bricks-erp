- 2026-10-12 — [backend, shared] — P9.1a **dispatch & delivery — challan, stock OUT on confirm, delivery acknowledgement, challan PDF, and LEK-04 gate reconciliation** (DSP-01, 02, 03, 05, LEK-04). Two tables, hand-written migration, 8 routes under `/dispatches`. A challan is raised as a draft, confirmed when the truck actually leaves (that is when stock moves, not when the paperwork is written), and signed for on delivery; a confirmed challan cannot be cancelled, because undoing it means putting bricks back in the yard and that is a stock event with its own reason. **The decision the module rests on is ADR-041: the bricks leave the yard exactly once.** P8 had already made the invoice deduct stock (ADR-038), so a challan that also deducted would have emptied the yard twice for one load — the most expensive silent error available here, since stock drifts down with no document to point at. Both orders of business are supported: invoice-first records the link on `dispatches.sales_invoice_id` and the challan moves nothing; challan-first records it on `sales_invoices.dispatch_id`, and the invoice bills what already moved with its lines pointing at the challan's own stock movements rather than writing new ones. `moved_stock` stores which happened instead of leaving it to be inferred, and a CHECK refuses the impossible pair. Billing more than the truck carried is `INVOICE_EXCEEDS_CHALLAN`; billing less is allowed, because part-billing is ordinary, and LEK-04 flags it. **LEK-04** puts three numbers beside each confirmed challan — dispatched, invoiced, and what the stock ledger actually recorded — and counts the trucks that left with no bill at all, which is the leak the client described. Permissions are three codes rather than two: `dispatch.manage` opens the gate and therefore moves stock, `dispatch.acknowledge` only records who signed for the load, and the accountant holds `dispatch.view` alone so they reconcile the gate without being able to open it; the Driver role stays empty in CP1 because a driver is master data, not a user. Confirm takes a per-challan advisory lock, so eight simultaneous confirms deduct the yard exactly once (ADR-027). The challan PDF says on its face that it is not a tax invoice. 28 new e2e (476 → **530**, 18 suites) and 21 new schema-integrity assertions (**196**). — DSP-01, DSP-02, DSP-03, DSP-05, LEK-04

- 2026-10-12 — [backend, shared] — P7.1 **purchases engine — PO → GRN → bill → payment → return, with payables ageing** (PUR-01…06, GST-03). Ten tables, hand-written migration. A goods receipt moves **quantity only** through `StockService.record(manager, …)` in its own transaction — ACCOUNTING_RULES puts value on the bill, so a GRN posts no journal — and it is the P7 instance of INV-05. The bill posts through `PostingService` with input CGST/SGST/IGST on their own ledgers, because input GST is a claimable credit rather than part of the purchase cost; each line carries its own rates, since one bill can span HSN codes, and a line charging both CGST+SGST and IGST is refused in the schema as well as the service. Payment is **bill-wise**: an allocation cannot exceed what a bill still owes, the same bill cannot appear twice in one payment, and the supplier's bills are locked for the transaction so eight simultaneous payments cannot together overpay one bill — proved by a concurrency test where exactly four of eight succeed and the bill lands exactly paid (ADR-027). A return writes stock OUT and the mirror debit note in one transaction, so returning stock already consumed is refused by ADR-026 and the whole document rolls back. Ageing buckets each unpaid bill from its due date, falling back to the bill date when no terms were given. `purchases.manage` covers raising and receiving; **paying needs `accounts.manage` too**, so a store keeper may receive goods and enter the bill but releasing the cash is somebody else's signature. 21 new e2e (426 total) and 25 new schema-integrity assertions (144). — PUR-01, PUR-02, PUR-03, PUR-04, PUR-05, PUR-06, GST-03
