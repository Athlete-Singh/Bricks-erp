- 2026-10-08 — [backend] — P2.1c the books (ACC-05): Day Book (vouchers in a date range with their entries, paged), Ledger statement (opening balance folded from everything before the range, each entry with a running balance on the ledger's **natural side** — a credit on an income ledger reads positive, not negative), and Trial Balance (closing per ledger, grouped, with Σ Dr vs Σ Cr proved and the difference reported when opening balances do not tie).
